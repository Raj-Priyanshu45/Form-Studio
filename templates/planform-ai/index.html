01 / WHY IT EXISTSMORE DESIGN. LESS CLEANUP.

Software should
do more of the
busywork.

Modeling tools are powerful, but repetitive coordination and rule-based checks can steal time from design. Planform is a fictional AI-product concept that observes the model, explains possible issues, and keeps the designer in control of what happens next.

02 / THE WORKFLOWOBSERVE → ADVISE → ACT → LEARN

Helpful, not
hands-off.

The agent shows its reasoning and asks before editing. The interaction below is illustrative, not connected to a BIM engine.

01 / OBSERVE

Keep context

Read model geometry and flag changes that could need attention.

02 / ADVISE

Explain the issue

Turn a dense rule into a clear, contextual note and suggested next step.

03 / ACT

Ask before changing

Review a proposed edit and explicitly accept or dismiss it.

04 / LEARN

Respect standards

Represent project-specific guidance without removing designer oversight.
